7 Ways to Play

Choose your mode — from Mercy Rule to Silent Street.

1. Mercy Rule

Stakes: None

No real money. No real promises. Imaginary help only. Best for beginners and parties.

2. Standard

Stakes: Promises

Real promises (within 48 hours). No money. Perfect for regular game nights.

3. Full Street

Stakes: Money + Promises

Real promises + real money. Winner takes all. For close friends and high stakes.

4. Exposure Mode

Stakes: Personal

Players write names before the game. Challenges from a bowl. Groups with history.

5. Courtroom Mode

Stakes: Authority

One Judge. No voting. Judge's word is final. Best for large groups.

6. Speed Street

Stakes: Speed

15-second phases. Whistle every 15 seconds. Quick, intense games.

7. Silent Street

Stakes: Intensity

No speaking during defense. Written defense only. For advanced players.

The Rules

The street remembers everything.

🏛️ Street Rules (5 Commandments)

  • 1. Your word is your bond.
  • 2. The street remembers everything.
  • 3. No fake friends.
  • 4. The street never forgets.
  • 5. Pay your debts.

🔄 The 5 Phases

  • 1. The Problem (30s) — Read the card. Say "Who can help?"
  • 2. The Challenge (1m) — Slam the gavel. Say "THE STREET REMEMBERS."
  • 3. The Pledge (30s) — Say WHAT, WHEN, and what you WANT in return.
  • 4. The Choice (15s) — Pick a helper. Give 2 tokens. Take 1 collateral.
  • 5. The Whistle — Round ends. Next player becomes "Help Wanted."

📊 Reputation System

  • Gain: Help someone (+1), Found INNOCENT (+1), Volunteer (+1)
  • Lose: Fail to help (-2), Found GUILTY (-1), False accusation (-1), Caught lying (-3)
  • CANCELLED at -3: Lose all tokens. Out of the game.
  • EXILED at -4 or lower: Cannot play for 3 game nights.
